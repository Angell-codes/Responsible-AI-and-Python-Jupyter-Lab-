⚖️ Responsible AI – Trimester 7  

Learning Responsible AI through real datasets and real impact. 
This repository compiles my experiments, notes, and code from Trimester 7, applying Responsible AI principles to diverse prediction tasks.  

📂 Datasets Explored
- 🩺 Diabetes (scikit-learn)  
- 🏠 California Housing (scikit-learn)  
- 🎗️ Breast Cancer (scikit-learn)  
- 📊 Adult Income dataset  
- ⚖️ Criminal dataset (African American bias case study)  

🔧 Methods & Insights
- Fairness metrics: SPD, DI, EOD, AOD  
- SHAP for transparency  
- FGSM adversarial robustness  
- Calibration with Brier score & curves  
- Conformal prediction & residual error modeling  

🌱 Beyond the Classroom
Studying conformal prediction led me to its **industrial relevance** — particularly in **fermentation processes**, where residual error models help optimize yields and monitor variability. This connection shows how Responsible AI principles extend beyond datasets into biotechnology and applied sciences.  

🚀 Purpose
This repo is a learning log: concise notes, reproducible notebooks, and reflections on fairness, robustness, privacy, and transparency — building a foundation for applying Responsible AI in both data science and bioprocess engineering.
